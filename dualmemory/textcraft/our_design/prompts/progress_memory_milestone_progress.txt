{TASK}

{GUIDE}

Current milestone: {CUR_NUM}/{NUM} (1-based)

{CUR_MILESTONE}

{INVENTORY}

Recent trajectory (most recent last). Each step has action and observation:
{TRAJECTORY}

Decision procedure:
1) Identify the current milestone's required target and count.
2) Check the inventory line and the recent trajectory from newest to oldest.
3) Find ONE exact quoted snippet that proves the milestone is completed, or conclude that it is not yet proven.
4) Be conservative: if the evidence is incomplete or ambiguous, do NOT advance.
5) You may either stay on the current milestone OR advance by exactly ONE milestone.

1) Use ONLY environment observations and the inventory line above as evidence.
2) Quote an exact substring for the evidence field. If not proven, evidence must be an empty string.

5) The JSON MUST include a short "thought_process" field that summarizes your reasoning steps.

How to interpret milestones:
- If a milestone says "... until ...", you may advance ONLY if the stopping condition ("until ...") is clearly satisfied.
- Otherwise, treat a milestone as complete when its key requirement is clearly satisfied, e.g.:
  - a successful "Got ..." observation for a required item
  - a successful "Crafted ..." observation for a required crafted item
  - the inventory line shows the required item count is reached
